Discover the Red Planet

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often called the Red Planet because of its reddish surface. With enormous volcanoes, deep canyons, and evidence of ancient water, Mars is one of the most fascinating places in our solar system.

NASA's robotic rovers explore the Martian surface, capturing images and collecting information to help scientists understand whether Mars could have supported life.

Fascinating Mars Facts

4th Planet

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun.

Red Planet

Iron-rich dust gives Mars its distinctive reddish color.

Two Moons

Mars has two small moons named Phobos and Deimos.

Olympus Mons

Mars is home to the largest known volcano in our solar system.
